# Streamer Career Simulator — Core Game Concept

## Main Description

The player takes on the role of an aspiring streamer, with the goal of building a career to achieve as much popularity and wealth as possible. At the beginning of the game, the player must choose their gender and nickname.

There are different paths to success. The player can become a friendly, drama-free streamer who attracts reputable sponsors, or a controversial streamer who follows the philosophy of "all publicity is good publicity" and accepts unusual or questionable sponsorship deals.

## Player Statistics

### Audience

One of the main statistics, included in the final score calculation.

Audience size affects the amount of money earned, access to better events, and income from sponsorship deals.

### Wealth (in PLN)

One of the main statistics, included in the final score calculation.

Money serves as the main currency in the game. Players can spend it on equipment and upgrades to improve the quality of their streams, as well as participate in various related events (e.g., buying a new webcam, microphone, etc.).

If the player's balance is negative, they cannot choose options that require spending money. These options will remain visible but will be disabled and unclickable.

### Drama Level

This statistic does not contribute to the final score.

Instead, it determines the types of events the player encounters. A low drama level may lead to more positive opportunities, such as launching your own pizza brand in Żabka, while a high drama level may attract questionable business deals and controversial sponsorships.

There is no single correct way to build a streaming career. Players can become either respected, drama-free creators or highly controversial personalities.

Real-life examples:
- **Completely drama-free:** Gucio
- **Extremely controversial:** Mammon

### Streaming Category Skill Levels

Before starting a stream, players can choose which category they want to stream in. Their skill level in that category affects the overall quality and potential success of the stream.

For example, if the player is highly skilled at FPS games, they have a better chance of attracting a larger audience and receiving invitations to category-specific events, such as world championships.

Players can also take risks by streaming categories they are unfamiliar with. Although their performance may initially be poor, they will gradually improve their skills through practice.

There is also a chance to create a viral clip during a stream. This could happen because of an incredible professional-level play or a hilarious failure that turns the streamer into a meme.

However, players must maintain variety in their content. Skills can decrease over time if they are not practiced, and repeatedly streaming the same category will eventually bore the audience, leading to stagnation in audience growth.

## Game Mechanics

The main decisions in the game will be resolved through dice rolls.

Player statistics influence the difficulty threshold and the probability of success.

There is also a chance of achieving a **critical success** or suffering a **critical failure**.

The system will work similarly to traditional tabletop RPG mechanics.

## Events

### Before the Stream

Players will encounter various situations that require decisions before starting a stream.

Examples:
- Receiving a sponsorship offer for an upcoming stream.
- Another streamer mentions or criticizes the player, forcing them to decide how to respond.

These decisions may provide temporary bonuses to streaming statistics, such as increased revenue or audience growth, or result in negative consequences.

### During the Stream

Various unexpected situations may occur while the player is live.

Examples:
- Receiving a controversial donation.
- Being stream-sniped by other players.
- Accidentally clicking a suspicious link.
- Accidentally leaking private information.

As with pre-stream events, the player's decisions can positively or negatively affect the stream's performance, including earnings and audience growth.

### During the Week

Between streams, players will encounter additional events and opportunities.

Examples:
- Buying a new webcam.
- Purchasing a better microphone.
- Creating an OnlyFans account (or uploading new content if the account already exists).
- Being recognized and approached by viewers in public.

These decisions can provide bonuses or penalties to streaming performance, finances, and audience growth.

## Ending the Career

A streaming career can last a maximum of **20 years**, although players can choose to retire at any point.

However, the player must be careful, as their career can also end prematurely due to certain conditions:

- **Permanent platform ban:** Certain decisions and events may result in the player being permanently banned from the streaming platform, immediately ending their career.
- **Bankruptcy:** If the player's balance reaches **-1,000 PLN**, the game automatically ends in defeat.

### Career Summary

At the end of the career, the player receives a summary screen containing:

- **Final score:** Calculated using a formula based on the player's total wealth and audience size.
- **Career summary:** A personalized description generated from predefined templates based on the player's final statistics and decisions.
- **Career diploma:** The player can generate a PDF certificate commemorating their streaming career and achievements.

### Leaderboard

We could also introduce a leaderboard system.

If the player's final score ranks among the **top 100**, they will be allowed to enter their initials or nickname (maximum 5 characters), similar to the high-score systems found in classic arcade games.
