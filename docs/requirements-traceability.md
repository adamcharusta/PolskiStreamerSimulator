# Requirements Traceability

Status: **living matrix**, created 2026-10-12 for milestone M1 ([#1](https://github.com/adamcharusta/PolskiStreamerSimulator/issues/1)). Each row maps a requirement to its source specification, its observable acceptance criterion and the issue or milestone that delivers it. Rows `REQ-01`…`REQ-27` follow the order of the [feature acceptance matrix](validation-and-delivery.md#2-feature-acceptance-matrix); `REQ-28` and `REQ-29` come from the [technical architecture](technical-architecture.md). Milestones follow [validation and delivery §1](validation-and-delivery.md#1-scope-and-release-stages): M1 simulation prototype, M2 vertical slice, M3 system complete, M4 online and administration, M5 content and balance lock, M6 public release.

Status values: `Planned` (not started), `In progress (Mn)` (part of the requirement is being delivered in milestone n; the remainder stays with the named later milestone), `Implemented (Mn)` (the milestone-n part has passing tests). M1 and M2 were released to `main` and closed on 2026-10-16 ([#68](https://github.com/adamcharusta/PolskiStreamerSimulator/pull/68)); every row they touched is `Implemented (M1)` or `Implemented (M2)`, with any remainder named in its issue column.
